Chillable, easygoing rosés made for warm afternoons and casual hosting. These are the bottles you reach for without overthinking, for summer weekends that aren't meant to be complicated.

Crisp & Coastal

Saline, mineral, and clean-edged rosés shaped by coast, limestone, or elevation. These drink particularly well alongside seafood and lighter summer meals that benefit from a focused, refreshing pour.

For the Table

Structured, savory rosés with enough depth for grilled fish, roast chicken, charcuterie, and summer vegetables. These are dinner rosés, made to be opened at the table, not just on the way to it.

Shop For the Table →

Serious Rosé

Site-specific, textural, and producer-driven bottles that make the case for rosé beyond the season. Age-worthy, food-compatible, and built on terroir rather than occasion. Worth paying attention to.
